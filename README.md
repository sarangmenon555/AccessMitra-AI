# AccessMitra AI

Helping people find and understand official information about disability-related schemes, rights, education and support services in India.

## Overview

AccessMitra AI lets you ask a question in everyday language and directs you to the official sources that answer it. Each answer explains in plain language what the source says, what is still unclear and what you should check with the responsible authority before acting.

AccessMitra AI is an information-navigation service. It does not determine legal rights, certify disability, guarantee eligibility, approve applications or replace government authorities and qualified professionals. It is not a government service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government department unless this is explicitly documented.

## The problem

Support for persons with disabilities in India is spread across legislation, rules, scheme guidelines, application portals, department notices and state-level programmes. Each has its own language, conditions and update cycle. For persons with disabilities and their families, this creates real barriers:

- **Scattered information.** A single need, such as an assistive device, a scholarship or a disability certificate, can involve several departments and portals.
- **Difficult language.** Legal and administrative text is hard to read, and a small misunderstanding can lead to a rejected application or a missed benefit.
- **Details that change.** Eligibility rules, required documents and deadlines are revised, and older summaries online are often out of date.
- **No clear starting point.** Many people do not know which authority, scheme or document applies to them.
- **Misinformation.** Unofficial pages and social media posts can present outdated or incorrect claims as fact.
- **Privacy risk.** Services that ask for identity numbers or medical documents up front put sensitive information at risk.

## How AccessMitra AI helps

- **Starts from official sources.** Questions are matched against a curated register of official sources, so answers begin with the responsible authority and not with guesswork.
- **Explains in plain language.** Each answer summarises what the source says in everyday terms and separates what the source states from interpretation.
- **Shows where to verify.** Every substantive answer links to the official source it draws on, with the issuing authority and the relevant section where available.
- **Admits uncertainty.** If reliable evidence is missing, outdated or conflicting, the answer says so and does not guess about rights or eligibility.
- **Makes the status of each answer clear.** Answers are labelled in words as supported by the source, a possible match, in need of clarification, based on conflicting sources or not verified.
- **Is open about its sources.** A public register shows each source, its issuing authority and whether a person has checked it, along with the date of that check.
- **Protects privacy.** It does not ask for Aadhaar numbers, disability certificate numbers or medical records, and it rejects messages that appear to contain such identifiers.
- **Welcomes correction.** Anyone can report an incorrect or outdated answer, a broken official link or an accessibility barrier.

## Who it is for

- Persons with disabilities looking for schemes, rights and opportunities
- Family members and caregivers
- Educators, counsellors and community workers who support them
- Organisations that want to understand how official information can be made easier to navigate

## What you can do

- Ask a question about schemes, rights, education or support services, optionally choosing your state or Union Territory
- Follow links to official sources and read them directly
- Browse the source register to see what the service relies on and how recently each source was checked
- Report errors, broken links and accessibility barriers

## Feedback

Use the Report an issue page on the website, or open an issue in this repository. Please do not include Aadhaar numbers, medical documents, disability certificate numbers or other sensitive personal information.

## Disclaimer

AccessMitra AI provides information-navigation assistance only. It does not make official decisions or guarantee eligibility. Rules, deadlines and requirements can change, so always check the responsible authority's current instructions.
